## Opinion on the original lockup

`MBOYA` is set much heavier and tighter than `ADVOCATES`, so the wordmark reads as two unrelated lines rather than one firm name. The LLP rules are slightly longer than the type they sandwich. The MK + leaf mark is the strongest asset — especially the leaf as the K’s upper arm — and should lead small sizes (favicon, social). Do not replace the original; keep it as primary source and use the refined/inverted treatments on dark UI.

## Taken from MyCFO vs original

**From MyCFO / Leemify:** overlay nav, full-viewport dark hero, Plus Jakarta Sans, turquoise pill button + ghost outline, numbered/card section rhythm, dark footer band, molecule/bubble graphic language, colour tokens.

**Original to this firm:** MK + leaf DNA, outline copy, practice-area IA, founder-led framing, no payroll product UI, no invented bios.
